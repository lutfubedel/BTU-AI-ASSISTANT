**2020-2021 Incoming Academic Staff Application Procedure**

Application Requirements: Your university must have a signed Protocol with Bursa Technical University. Mobility for the 2020-2021 Academic year. Academic Staff from the list of partner universities below can apply in accordance with the Turkish Higher Education council list of priority fields:

- The duration for exchange is at least **two weeks** and at most **three months**.
- The scholarship includes a transportation grant and a daily grant. Please note that the grants are in Turkish Lira and that no additional payment will be made.
